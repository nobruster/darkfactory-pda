## Palette

- **Factory Black** #070A0F
- **Carbon** #111720
- **Graphite** #29313A
- **Proof Ivory** #F5F2EA
- **Forge Gold** #F3B64C (fold only — not the letters)

House surfaces: Factory Black, Carbon, Graphite, Proof Ivory, White. Settlement Mint and Verdict Coral are semantic state colors, not decorative brand accents.

## Rules

- Identity is Settlement Fold
- Letters are Proof Ivory on dark surfaces; Forge Gold lives on the fold triangle only
- Converge is the coordinator. It calls public binaries. It does not import Seamwise or Task-Spec
- Duplicate capability is tolerable. Duplicate authority is not
- Never Register Blue, Gate Ember, Ion Violet, Keep Rose, Datum Lime, or sibling product accents
