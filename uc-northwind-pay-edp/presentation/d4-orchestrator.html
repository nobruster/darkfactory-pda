Loop Engineering · /goal · four engines

The loop is /goal.

A prompt is one turn. A goal is a finish line. Write a verifiable condition, and the agent keeps taking turns until a check says yes — without you hitting Enter.

/loop
Clock

Next turn starts when a timer fires. Poll CI, a quiet PR, a deploy. You stop it, or the agent decides the work is done.

/goal
Condition

Next turn starts when the last one finishes. Stops when a check confirms the condition holds. That is the loop.
