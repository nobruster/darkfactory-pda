## Palette

- **Factory Black** #070A0F
- **Carbon** #111720
- **Graphite** #29313A
- **Proof Ivory** #F5F2EA
- **Ion Violet** #A56BFF (ticks only — first three longer; second tick is Outcome)

House surfaces: Factory Black, Carbon, Graphite, Proof Ivory. Ion Violet is the brand accent on the ticks, not the letters.

## Rules

- Identity is Seven Cue
- Seven ticks, fixed Outcome Brief order: Status → Outcome → Human action → Proof → Gaps → Next → Open
- Ion Violet lives on the ticks only. Second tick is Outcome. Never last.
- Letters are Proof Ivory on dark surfaces; Factory Black on light
- Brief-Spec standardizes the explanation and handoff, not the agent's reasoning
- Never calls a model
- Never Forge Gold, Proof Gold, Gate Ember, Register Blue, Keep Rose, Datum Lime, or sibling product accents
